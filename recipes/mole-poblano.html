Mole poblano

Ingredientes

  • 2 dried pasilla chiles
  • 2 dried mulato chiles
  • 2 dried ancho chiles
  • 1/4 cup of sesame seeds
  • 1/4 cup of almonds
  • 1/4 cup of raisins
  • 1/4 cup of pumpkin seeds
  • 1/4 cup of peanuts
  • 1/4 cup of chocolate (preferably Mexican chocolate)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon of cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon of cumin
  • 1 teaspoon of coriander
  • 1 teaspoon of black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons of lard or vegetable oil
  • 4 cups of chicken broth
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Remove the stems and seeds from the dried chiles. Toast them lightly in a dry skillet for a few seconds until they become fragrant. Be careful not to burn them.
  2. In the same skillet, toast the sesame seeds, almonds, raisins, pumpkin seeds, and peanuts until they are golden brown. Set aside.
  3. In a blender or food processor, combine the toasted chiles, toasted nuts and seeds, chocolate, onion, garlic, cinnamon, cumin, coriander, and black pepper. Blend until you get a smooth paste.
  4. In a large saucepan, heat the lard or vegetable oil over medium heat. Add the chile paste and cook for a few minutes, stirring constantly.
  5. Gradually add the chicken broth while stirring to prevent lumps. Bring to a simmer and let it cook for about 20-30 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  6. Season with salt to taste. Serve the mole poblano over cooked chicken or turkey and garnish with sesame seeds if desired.
  7. Enjoy your delicious mole poblano!
