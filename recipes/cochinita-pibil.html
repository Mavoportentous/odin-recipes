Cochinita Pibil

Ingredientes

  • 1 kg of pork (preferably leg or loin)
  • 2 tablespoons of achiote paste
  • 1/2 cup of bitter orange juice (or a mixture of orange and lime/lemon juice)
  • 1/4 cup of white vinegar
  • 4 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon of ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon of dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Banana leaves (optional, for wrapping the meat)
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced ​​(for serving)
  • 1 habanero chili, finely chopped (optional, for serving)
  • Corn tortillas (for serving)

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, mix the achiote, bitter orange juice, vinegar, garlic, cumin, oregano, salt and pepper to make the marinade.
  1. Add the pork to the marinade and make sure it's well coated. Cover the container and let it marinate in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight .
  2. Preheat the oven to 160°C (320°F).
  3. If you are using banana leaves, blanch them in hot water to soften them and then place them on a baking sheet.
  4. Place the marinated meat on the banana leaves (if using) or directly on the baking sheet. Cover with additional banana leaves if necessary.
  5. Cover the baking sheet with aluminum foil and bake for approximately 3-4 hours, or until the meat is tender and shreds easily.
  6. Remove from the oven and let it rest for a few minutes before shredding the meat.
  7. Serve the cochinita pibil in corn tortillas, accompanied by red onion and habanero chili if desired.
  8. Enjoy your delicious cochinita pibil!
