Pozole Rojo

Ingredientes

For the soup base:

  • 3 lbs (approx. 1.3 kg) boneless pork shoulder or pork mix (shoulder and ribs), cubed
  • 10 cups water or chicken/pork broth
  • 1 head of garlic, halved
  • 1 white onion, halved
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp salt (adjust to taste)
  • 3 cans (15 oz each) white hominy, drained and rinsed

For the red chile sauce (adobo):

  • 4 to 5 dried guajillo chiles (stemmed and seeded)
  • 2 to 3 dried ancho chiles (stemmed and seeded)
  • 1/2 white onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp dried Mexican oregano
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil or lard (for frying the sauce)
  • Salt to taste

Traditional toppings / Garnishes:

  • Shredded iceberg lettuce or green cabbage
  • Thinly sliced radishes
  • Draped or chopped white onion
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • Lime wedges
  • Dried Mexican oregano
  • Tostadas

Instructions

  1. Cook the Pork: In a large stockpot, combine the cubed pork, water (or broth), halved garlic head, halved onion, bay leaves, and salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer for about 1.5 hours until the pork is tender.
  2. Prepare the Chiles: While the pork cooks, place the dried guajillo and ancho chiles in a small saucepan, cover them with water, and bring to a boil. Simmer for about 5 to 10 minutes until soft and pliable.
  3. Blend the Sauce: Transfer the softened chiles to a blender. Add the 1/2 white onion, 2 garlic cloves, dried oregano, cumin, a pinch of salt, and about 1 cup of the warm chile-soaking water. Blend until completely smooth. For a smoother texture, pass the sauce through a fine-mesh strainer.
  4. Fry the Sauce: In a separate small skillet, heat 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil or lard over medium heat. Pour in the strained chile sauce and fry for 5 minutes, stirring constantly until it darkens slightly and thickens.
  5. Combine Everything: Add the fried red chile sauce and the drained, rinsed white hominy into the large pot with the tender pork and its broth. Stir well, cover, and let it simmer for another 30 minutes so the flavors meld together. Taste and add more salt if needed.
  6. Serve: Ladle the hot pozole into deep bowls. Serve with bowls of shredded cabbage/lettuce, radishes, chopped onion, cilantro, lime wedges, dried oregano, and crispy tostadas on the side so everyone can garnish their own bowl.
